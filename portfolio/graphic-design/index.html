    Free 20-minute call

    We talk about your business, your customers and what the design needs to do. You get a fixed quote within 24 hours.

    Deposit and brief

    A 50% deposit books your slot. I send a one-page brief so we agree on goals before any design starts.

About

Questions clients ask

How much does a project cost?

Identity work starts at $900, campaigns at $350 and packaging at $450. You get a fixed quote after our call, so the price never changes halfway through.

How long does it take?

Campaigns take 1–2 weeks, identities 3–4 weeks. If you have a hard launch date, tell me on the form and I'll say honestly whether it's possible.

How many revisions do I get?

Two full rounds are included. Most clients don't need both, because we agree on the brief before design starts.

Do you use AI?

I use it to explore ideas and mock up scenes quickly. Logos, layouts and type are drawn and set by hand, and you own full commercial rights to everything delivered.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. About half my clients are in the UK and US. I invoice in USD or GBP and we meet over Google Meet or WhatsApp.
