Questions people ask before booking

How much will my project cost?

Identity work starts at $900, flyers and social at $350, packaging at $450. You get a fixed quote after our call, and it doesn't change halfway through.

How long does it take?

Flyers take one to two weeks, identities three to four. If you have a launch date, put it in the form and I'll tell you honestly whether it's possible.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two full rounds are included. Most clients don't need both, because we agree on the brief before design starts.

Do you use AI?

For exploring ideas and mocking up scenes, yes. Logos, layouts and type are drawn and set by hand, and you own full commercial rights to everything I deliver.

Can you work with me if I'm outside Nigeria?

Yes. About half my clients are in the UK and US. I invoice in dollars or pounds and we talk on Google Meet or WhatsApp.
